How virtual planning works

Every plan follows the same four steps, so nothing gets missed without a site visit.

1

Intake and homework

You fill out a short questionnaire and send your ground truth: a phone video walk of key areas, photos, and trail cam pictures.

2

Kickoff call

We spend about 90 minutes on a video call, looking at your maps together while you give me the tour. We cover history, goals, budget, equipment, neighbors, and how you hunt.

3

Design

I study soils, topography, cover and timber types, ridges, pinch points, and water. Every plan cross-references three sources of imagery for accuracy.

4

Delivery and follow-up

You get plan maps (PDF), a video walkthrough, and a written implementation plan with a timeline. A 30-minute follow-up call answers what comes up once you’ve read it.

Questions

Can a virtual plan really work without a site visit?

Yes, when it’s built right. I combine soils, topography, cover types, and three imagery sources with the ground truth you send. You also know your land’s history in ways a single visit can’t capture, and the follow-up call lets us fine-tune.

What do I need to send you?

Your property address or boundary, the intake questionnaire, a phone video walk, photos, and trail cam pictures if you have them.

Will I be able to do the work myself?

Yes. Every plan is written for landowners to follow on their own timeline, phased by priority and budget.

Where do you work?

Anywhere whitetails live.

Do you still offer on-site visits?

Not at this time. Working virtually lets me help landowners in every state, at a lower cost than a site visit.
